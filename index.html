// 01 — PURPOSE

A MAP OF YOUR AI KNOWLEDGE

AI conversations often become scattered across products, exports, and separate histories. Contextual explores a unified way to search and connect that material, track how topics evolve, and preserve useful context without presenting a marketing page as proof of a working backend.
